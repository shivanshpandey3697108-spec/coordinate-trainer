Learn

Core rules

  • Latitude: 0° to 90° from the Equator. N is positive, S is negative.
  • Longitude: 0° to 180° from the Prime Meridian. E is positive, W is negative.
  • 1° of latitude ≈ 111 km. 1° of longitude ≈ 111 km × cos(latitude).
  • 15° of longitude = 1 hour of time.

Method

  1. Use the signs to pick the quadrant.
  2. Match longitude to an anchor meridian to find the continent.
  3. Match latitude to a band to find the climate zone.
  4. Find the nearest anchor city and count the degrees away.
  5. Multiply by 111 km to check the distance.
